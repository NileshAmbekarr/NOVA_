**Training & Placement Cell (TPC):**
- The college has a dedicated and active Training & Placement Cell.
- The TPC is headed by a Training & Placement Officer (TPO) who coordinates all placement activities.
- The cell works year-round to connect students with industry opportunities.

**Placement Process:**
1. **Registration:** Final-year students register with the placement cell at the beginning of the academic year.
2. **Pre-Placement Training:** Aptitude training, group discussion practice, mock interviews, soft skills workshops, and technical preparation sessions are conducted.
3. **Company Invitation:** The TPC invites companies for campus recruitment drives.
4. **Pre-Placement Talk (PPT):** Companies give a presentation about their organization, job roles, salary packages, and expectations.
5. **Written Test / Online Assessment:** Aptitude test (quantitative, logical, verbal), technical test, and coding test.
6. **Group Discussion (GD):** Some companies conduct GD rounds to assess communication and teamwork skills.
7. **Technical Interview:** Questions on core subjects, projects, and problem-solving skills.
8. **HR Interview:** Questions on personality, goals, strengths, weaknesses, and situational scenarios.
9. **Offer Letter:** Selected students receive offer letters from the company.
